SoilSutra turns published vermicompost research into short guides that a producer can use.

I am Vivek Madiwala, an engineer with a background in embedded systems and AI. I build the tools that search for, check and publish these guides, and I help vermicompost producers measure and control their process.

The guides are free. If you want help with your own site, write to me and tell me what you make and how much.
