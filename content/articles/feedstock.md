Livestock manures, compost, food scraps, and shredded or chopped cardboard or paper can serve as earthworm feedstocks. [1](#s1)

## Materials to feed or exclude

Bread and corn cobs can be fed to worms, but avoid onion, garlic, peppers and citrus fruit or peels. [4](#s4) Exclude plant material sprayed with insecticides or other chemicals from vermicomposting feedstocks. [3](#s3) Also exclude plastic, metal, and other non-biodegradable products from vermicomposting feedstocks. [3](#s3)

## Feedstock ratios

Eisenia fetida can thrive at feedstock C:N ratios around 50:1, compared with 25:1–30:1 for traditional composting. [2](#s2)

## Testing uncertain feedstocks

Before using an uncertain feedstock, test it in a container with about a dozen worms. [5](#s5) During this test, observe their behavior for 12 to 24 hours. [5](#s5)
